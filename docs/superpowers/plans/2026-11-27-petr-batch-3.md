## 1. Co-op: restart when everyone is down

"Potřebujeme na multiplayer restart pokud všichni umřeme, je otravné pořád [zakládat hru znovu]."

- Today a co-op run that is lost (all heroes down, or the castle) ends on the end screen and the group must open a
  new room. Reading: the host's end screen gets **Again** (same group, same course, a new seed): the host sends a
  new `start` over the links it already has (protocol: `start` may come again after `RunEnd`), the guests' end
  screen shows "Waiting for the host" and their window starts the new run like the first one.
- A guest who left is not in the new run; the host's lobby roster is the source. Each player's gear is read again
  (a guest resends `join` with his current loadout before the host's Again counts).
- Tests: `client/game/coop-setup` unit test of a second `start`; e2e with `?debug=1&mate=` (local co-op) that
  `again` after `down` for all starts a fresh run with the same heroes.

## 3. Co-op enemies weaker per player

"Při 3 lidech na normal bez gearu jsme nebyli schopni vyhrát vůbec nic ... Když jsme si pak dali komplet gear přes
cheat, tak jsme to zase zabili v pohodě."

- `rules.coop.perPlayer` today: enemy `hp` +0.9 and `damage` +0.45 per extra player (3 players: x2.8 hp, x1.9
  damage), `packSize` +0.3, `eliteHp` +0.25.
- Target (**open**): a group of 2-4 without gear wins about as often as one hero alone, the batch 2 target of about
  10 % autopilot wins (`npm run sim:run -- --hero all --seeds 1-20 --gear none --players 2-4`); full gear stays an
  easy win. Start from hp +0.5, damage +0.25 per extra player and tune with the sim; table in DECISIONS.
- Note: tasks 4 and 5 make heroes weaker per minute (fewer levels), so tune this after them.

## 4. Fewer spell levels

"Snížit levely spellů o 40 %, s tím že max levelů bude lvl 6 a ultimátky lvl 3. To platí pro ty minor buffy, ty
jdou do levelu 5. Potom už zbývá jen str / agi / int při levelu - to by se mohlo zobrazovat vždy v pořadí jak píšu."

- Signature spells and shared spells: 10 -> **6** levels. Ultimates: 2 -> **3** levels. Passives ("minor buffs"):
  10 -> **5** levels.
- Petr confirmed (2026-11-27): "spelly stejně silné jako dnes, jen ty kroky mezi nimi snížit". Level 1 and the top
  level keep today's values (level 1 and level 10, or 2 for ultimates), so a maxed spell is as strong as now; the
  levels between are spread again by the same rule (`levelGrowthExponent`, data-build's spread). An ultimate's new
  middle level is halfway.
- When every spell and passive is at its top (or every slot full and maxed), a level-up offers only the three
  attribute cards, always in the order **STR, AGI, INT** (also when they are mixed with other cards, the attribute
  cards come last in this order).
- Touches: `design/abilities*.yaml` / data-build spread (`levels`), `sim/cards*.ts` (offer), the HUD level pips and
  tooltips (level N / 6), `rules.debug.phaseXp`, tests that assume 10 levels, the determinism digest.
