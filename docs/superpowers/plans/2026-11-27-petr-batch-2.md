# Petr's batch 2 (2026-11-27): weaker heroes, casters, wards, blink, bosses, chest upgrades

> For agentic workers: one task = one commit (or a few), `npm run check` before each. Read only the files a task names.
> Petr's words are in the quotes (Czech); the readings and numbers after them are Claude's and are marked **open**
> where Petr should confirm them.

**Goal:** the game is too easy; heroes clear the screen before enemies reach them. Make the heroes weaker, the
enemies and bosses stronger and more varied, fix the shaman's wards and the blink, and let chests stop offering
items below what the hero wears. Target of the whole batch: **about 10 % autopilot wins without farmed items**.

**Order:** after stage 3 of the random items plan (`2026-11-27-random-items.md`), before its stage 4: these change
the feel of every run, and stages 4-6 (UI, balance, icons) should be built on the new numbers. Stage 5 of the random
items plan (balance) uses the target below.

## 0. The balance target (rule for every later balance change)

"Autopilot balancing by měl mít výher zhruba 10 % bez nafarmených itemů v levelu 1 - u všech hrdinů."

- `npm run sim:run -- --hero all --seeds 1-20 --gear none` (world 1 normal, from phase 1): every hero wins about
  10 % of the runs (2 of 20; accept 1-4 of 20 per hero, 5-15 % over all eight heroes).
- Record the table before and after every balance change in `docs/DECISIONS.md` (skill `balance-check`).
- The difficulty ladder keeps its order: Insane and Hell and the higher worlds stay harder than world 1 normal.

## 1. AoE of every spell -50 %

"Aoe všech spellů musíme snížit o 50 % hrdinové jsou moc silní."

- Reading: the radius of every hero area spell (nova, area, ticking area and its bursts, on-cast, cone length,
  frost ring, holy ground, meteor ...) x 0.5, through one multiplier `design/scaling.yaml` `abilities.areaScale: 0.5`
  applied by data-build to every `radius`, `hitRadius` and cone `range` param (cloud sessions: the data-change skill).
- **open:** radius x 0.5 makes the covered area a quarter. If Petr meant the area (not the radius), the radius is
  x 0.71. Claude's recommendation: start with radius x 0.5 (what was said), measure, then move to 0.6-0.7 if the
  autopilot falls far below 10 %.
- Gear's `areaSize` multiplies on top, as now.

## 2. A quarter of the enemies are ranged casters

"Chce to změnit pár melee enemy na ranged castery alespoň 25 % na castery."

- Today the factions have 2-3 ranged types of 5-6 (musketeers, archers, spearthrowers; healers and webcrawlers);
  most packs are melee. Goal: at least 25 % of the spawned common enemies (by phase mix weight) are **casters**:
  ranged enemies that throw a spell bolt (its own projectile look and sound per faction), stop at their range
  and keep it (behaviour `skirmish`).
- Per faction one melee type becomes a caster (or a new caster type joins the mix): human battle mage, orc shaman
  (already a healer: a new fire caller instead), undead necromancer acolyte, elf moon priestess. Own names (EN, CS),
  models from the KayKit sources (mage / skeleton mage), colours per faction.
- `rules.phases[].mix`: the caster share >= 25 % from phase 3 on (phases 1-2 stay mostly melee to teach).
- Test: the sum of the caster weights per phase >= 0.25 of the phase's mix.

## 3. Serpent Shaman's wards: 100 damage + 25 % per level

"Shaman mi pořád přijde, že má slabé wardy, dávají pořád jen 20 dmg a ne 100 na prvním levelu ... mají mít
100 dmg + 25 % každý level."

- Today Serpent Totem (`serpent-totem`, `params.damage`) bites for 20 at level 1, every 0.5 s, up to 101 at level 10.
- A totem's bite at level 1 = 100 (before spell power), each level +25 % of the level-1 value:
  100, 125, 150, ... 325 at level 10 (linear).
- **open:** or +25 % compounding (100, 125, 156, ... 745 at level 10). Recommendation: linear, because the
  compounding one makes level 10 seven times level 1, far above every other spell.
- Check that `levelGrowthExponent` does not flatten it (the ward row is written as design values) and that the
  tooltip shows 100 at level 1.

## 4. Blink (F): an instant teleport to the mouse, any distance

"Blink na F key je blbý, je to jen další dash ... má to být teleport z místa na místo pozice myši instantně na
jakoukoliv vzdálenost."

- F puts the hero at the mouse point in the same step: no travel, no `maxDistance` (only clamped to the arena and
  pushed out of obstacles), the stunning burst at the landing point stays, the cooldown stays 10 s.
- The input carries the mouse point in world units (it does already for the blink); co-op sends it in the input as
  now; the autopilot does not blink.
- Visual: a flash at the start and at the landing, no trail.

## 5. Bosses x3 hp, x2 damage, and their own names and looks per world

"Bossové jsou moc slabí, musíme jim zvednout hp na 3x a dmg na 2x. Plus pro každý level vymysli rozdílné
názvy a vizual."

- Every boss (the three minibosses and the final boss): hp x 3, damage x 2 (attack, slam, strike); through
  `design/enemies.yaml` multipliers, so the map's numbers stay traceable. Update the boss hp targets of
  `data/generated.test.ts`.
- Reading of "pro každý level": **each world has its own four bosses** with names and looks of their own (world 1
  keeps today's; worlds 2-5 get new ones in the colours of their world: a sand warlord, a grove colossus, a frost
  giant, a blight lord ...). Same mechanics per boss slot (charge, slam, strike), own model or tint, size, aura
  colour and name in EN and CS. `rules.drops` per boss slot, not per boss id.
- **open:** if "level" meant the difficulty (Normal / Insane / Hell) and not the world, the bosses get a new name
  prefix and a darker look per difficulty instead.

## 6. Chests: upgrades, or other slots in higher rarities

"Pokud už hrdinové budou mít nějakou raritu itemu na sobě ve hře, tak rollovat buď upgrady a nebo jiné typy
itemů ve vyšších raritách."

- A chest card for a slot that wears an item of rarity R offers either a level-up of the worn item (Level) or an
  item of a rarity above R (Choice upgrade); never another item of R or below.
- Rule: roll the rarity as now; for a worn slot whose rarity is at or above the rolled one, the card becomes a Level
  card of the worn item with `upgradeShare` (rules.loot, 0.5), else it moves to another slot (a free one or one with
  a lower rarity) where the rolled rarity is an upgrade; when no slot fits, it is a Level card.
- A mythic slot only ever gets Level cards.
- Tests: no Choice card offers a rarity at or below the worn one; the bot still picks upgrades first.
